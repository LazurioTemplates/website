We founded Example in 2019. We saw how much money companies spend on changes
nobody knows work: a new website, a discount campaign, another advertising
channel. We wanted to do it differently: test first, then invest.

## How we work

1. **Hypothesis.** Together we name what you want to improve and how we will
   know it worked.
2. **A small test.** We try the change on a part of your customers or on one
   page. The rest of your sales runs unchanged.
3. **Measurement.** We compare the result with what would have happened
   without the change. You see the numbers as we do.
4. **Decision.** We recommend rolling the change out, adjusting it or
   stopping it. The decision is always yours.

## Who we are

We are six people: analysts, developers and a project manager. We work from
our office in Prague and at our clients' across the country.

## What we do not do

- We do not promise guaranteed growth. We promise you will know what works.
- We do not buy reviews or ratings.
- We do not collect more data about your customers than measuring needs.
