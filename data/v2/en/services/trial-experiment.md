## What you get

- an experiment prepared and running, including changes to your website where
  needed;
- an ongoing overview of how the experiment is going;
- an evaluation with a recommendation to roll out, adjust or stop.

## How it works

A typical experiment takes six weeks: a week of preparation, four weeks of
running and a week of evaluation. If the change turns out to do harm on the
way, we stop the experiment at once.
