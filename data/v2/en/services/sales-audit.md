## What you get

- the customer's path from the first visit to the order, with the places
  where they leave;
- a list of five to ten changes ordered by their expected benefit;
- a first experiment to test the most important change.

## How it works

At a first meeting we talk about what you sell and to whom. Then we get access
to your website statistics and orders. Two weeks later we present the results
at a second meeting and leave them with you in writing.
