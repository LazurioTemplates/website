In the first half of the year we evaluated 38 experiments with our clients. 13
of them paid off, about a third. That is a usual share: most ideas that look
good in a meeting do not change sales.

## What helped

- **Clearer prices.** A price with VAT and delivery right at the product
  raised the number of orders in five of seven tests.
- **A shorter checkout.** Fewer required fields helped in four of six tests.

## What did not help

New discount campaigns brought more visits but not more profit. Only one of
eight paid off.
